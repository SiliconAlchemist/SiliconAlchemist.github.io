## Context

Software Engineer III · Walmart Global Tech · Bengaluru, Karnataka · June 2022–November 2024.

My work at Walmart Global Tech covered the data lifecycle from high-volume ingestion and transformation to the APIs and interfaces through which people consumed reporting.

## ETL at eight terabytes a day

I designed and implemented ETL workflows that integrated and transformed **8 TB of data daily**. The work established the data-processing foundation for downstream products while operating at production scale.

## Direct Kafka ingestion

I rebuilt the ingestion pipeline to consume directly from Apache Kafka instead of relying on multiple intermediary data stores. The new architecture improved data freshness from **two days to two hours** and reduced cloud costs by **30%**.

## Reporting platform from journey to launch

I independently built a reporting platform and delivered its first release within **two months**. My ownership included user-journey mapping and implementation across the data, API, and frontend layers, connecting product decisions with end-to-end engineering delivery.
