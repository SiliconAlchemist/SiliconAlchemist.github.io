## Overview

I designed a deterministic cloud risk engine at Oracle to prioritize emerging faults and alert customers across three cloud services. The engine incorporated event calibration, metric anomalies, forecasts, and operational events.

## My contribution

The design combined several kinds of operational evidence into a risk-prioritization system. Unlike the anomaly detection project, which focuses on identifying unusual metric behavior, this project focuses on how those signals contribute to emerging-fault prioritization and customer alerts.
