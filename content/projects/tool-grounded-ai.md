## Context

Senior Data Systems Software Engineer · Oracle · Bengaluru, Karnataka · November 2024–present.

My work at Oracle spans intelligent monitoring, incident investigation, cloud-risk prioritization, and developer tooling. These systems share a common goal: turning complex operational information into decisions that engineers, leaders, and customers can act on.

## Infrastructure anomaly detection

I engineered an anomaly detection system for infrastructure metrics that processes more than **25 million observations each hour**. It combines statistical methods with custom-trained LSTM models to identify unusual behavior across high-volume operational signals.

## Natural-language cloud data exploration

I built a platform that uses MCP integrations and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to help engineers and executives investigate incidents and query cloud-service data through natural language. The experience was designed to support different audiences asking different questions of the same operational systems.

## Deterministic cloud risk engine

I designed a deterministic risk engine that combines event calibration, metric anomalies, forecasts, and operational events. It prioritizes emerging faults and supports customer alerting across three cloud services, connecting individual signals to a broader assessment of operational risk.

## Coding agents for APEX development

I integrated autonomous coding agents into the APEX development workflow so teammates could maintain and extend the platforms more efficiently. The integration reduced development and maintenance time by **50%**.
