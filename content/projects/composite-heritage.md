## About the work

Composite Heritage is a visual project published in 2020 that brings together creative direction and animation around themes of heritage and connection.

The Behance case study includes *The Thread of Composite Heritage* and the #knowyourheritage message. Its presentation uses After Effects, Premiere Pro, Illustrator, and Photoshop.
