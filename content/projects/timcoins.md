## About the work

TimCoins is a mobile-app concept for productivity and time management. Its Behance case study frames time through creative deadlines, gamification, and the materialisation of time.

## Presentation

The project is presented through UI screens and product-design views. The Behance project is categorized under UI/UX, illustration, and product design, and lists Illustrator, XD, and Photoshop among its tools.
